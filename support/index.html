About the Game

Joker Blast is a casual game collection that includes classic games like Minesweeper, Link Match, Solitaire, and more — all in one offline, ad-free experience. No internet required, no interruptions, just pure fun.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Q: Do I need an internet connection to play?
    A: No. The entire game works offline.
  • Q: Are there any ads?
    A: No ads at all. Joker Blast is a completely ad-free experience.
  • Q: Is this app free to use?
    A: Yes, the app is free to download and play.
  • Q: Does the app collect any personal data?
    A: Please refer to our Privacy Policy for full details, but in short — we do not collect any personally identifiable data.
  • Q: What devices are supported?
    A: The app supports iPhones and iPads running iOS 13.0 or later.
