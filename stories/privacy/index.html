Privacy Policy

Safe, gentle, and ad-free play for our youngest minds. Learn how we prioritize kids' privacy in Together Bedtime Stories.

Application: Together Bedtime Stories
Effective Date: May 25, 2026

Welcome to Together Bedtime Stories. We build gentle, high-contrast sensory toys designed to provide a completely safe environment for infants and toddlers. This Privacy Policy details our practices for data collection, usage, and retention.

1. Information We Collect

We operate on absolute child-privacy principles. We do not require registration, custom profiles, or track online behavior.

  • No Account Required: You can play Together Bedtime Stories fully offline without entering any personal information (names, emails, age, or phone numbers).
  • Purchases: We offer optional in-app purchases processed securely via Google Play Billing. We never collect or see your payment card numbers.
  • Information You Voluntarily Provide: If you contact us for parent support or to submit feedback, we collect your email address and any support query details you submit.
  • Automatically Collected Diagnostics: To ensure that the sensory graphics render smoothly across all device sizes, we may collect technical hardware specs (device manufacturer, CPU type, OS version, and app performance logs). This contains no personally identifiable information (PII).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use this information strictly for the following purposes:

  • To ensure proper technical performance and layout compatibility.
  • To diagnose, resolve, and repair system bugs or crash anomalies.
  • To respond to and troubleshoot any customer support requests.

👶 Family & Child Safe Play

Together Bedtime Stories is completely ad-free. We do not have user tracking databases, advertising profiles, or custom telemetry servers. Your baby's sensory interaction remains fully on your own local device.

3. Data Retention and Deletion Policy

We have instituted a transparent data retention protocol to ensure we do not retain information longer than required for operational maintenance.

Data Type Storage Location Retention Duration Purpose & Deletion Method
Gameplay Progress & Local Achievements On-Device Local Storage Indefinite (controlled by you) Stores game status locally. Removing the application or clearing storage instantly deletes this data permanently.
Technical Logs & Crash Reports Secure Developer Console Platforms (Google Play Console) Maximum of 90 days Helps us resolve bugs. Automatically deleted or fully anonymized after 90 days.
Customer Support Communications Secure Email Server Archive Maximum of 180 days Maintains support history. Deleted permanently within 180 days after resolution of the query, unless required for legal compliance.

If you want us to manually delete your email thread before our 180-day archive expiration, please submit a deletion request to the support contact in Section 8.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data. Anonymized logs are only shared with trusted platforms (e.g. Google Developer Services) to detect performance bugs, and they are legally required to keep this data safe. We may also disclose data in rare cases under strict legal obligations.

5. Children's Privacy

Together Bedtime Stories is built for parents and families. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If any parent discovers their child under 13 has emailed us, please notify us, and we will delete that message and email address immediately.

6. Your Choices and Rights

No matter where you live, you have the right to request access, correction, or deletion of any email support messages you have sent us. We will fulfill all valid requests within 30 days.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy to reflect new development designs or compliance rules. We will update the effective date at the top. We encourage parents to review this page periodically.
