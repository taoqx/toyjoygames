Privacy Policy

Your personal finances and receipts belong strictly to you. Learn how we protect your privacy in Pocket Receipt.

Application: Pocket Receipt
Effective Date: November 19, 2026

Welcome to Pocket Receipt. We develop lightweight, private, and intuitive utility tools that help you organize and track expenses and receipts with complete peace of mind. This Privacy Policy details our practices regarding data collection, usage, and retention.

1. Information We Collect

We adhere to a strict local-first privacy standard. We do not require registration, online user accounts, or cloud profiling.

  • No Account Required: You can use Pocket Receipt completely offline without creating an account or providing personal details (such as your full name, phone number, or home address).
  • Receipts & Expense Data: All receipts, photographed invoices, expense amounts, merchant names, categories, tags, and dates you record are stored locally on your device's sandbox storage. We never upload your receipt images or expense history to external cloud servers.
  • Camera & Storage Permissions: The application may request access to your device camera and photo gallery solely to allow you to snap new receipt photos or import existing receipt documents into your local records. Photos and files remain strictly on your local device.
  • Purchases: We may offer optional in-app purchases or subscriptions processed securely through Google Play Billing. We never collect, process, or store your credit card or bank credentials.
  • Information You Voluntarily Provide: If you contact our support desk for technical assistance or feedback, we collect your email address and any support query details you submit.
  • Automatically Collected Diagnostics: To ensure that Pocket Receipt runs smoothly across all screen sizes and Android OS versions, we may receive anonymized technical diagnostics (such as device model, OS version, and crash logs provided via Google Play Console). This contains no personally identifiable information (PII).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use this information strictly for the following purposes:

  • To provide local expense calculation, category management, and receipt photo organization on your device.
  • To diagnose, resolve, and repair software glitches, bugs, or crash events.
  • To reply to and troubleshoot customer inquiries and support requests.

🧾 Local-First Financial Privacy

Pocket Receipt operates with an offline-first architecture. We do not maintain remote databases of your financial records, receipts, or spending habits. Your personal budget details remain 100% on your own device.

3. Data Retention and Deletion Policy

We maintain a clear and transparent data retention policy to ensure data is never retained longer than necessary for operational and support purposes.

Data Type Storage Location Retention Duration Purpose & Deletion Method
Receipt Photos & Expense Records On-Device Local Storage Indefinite (user-controlled) Stores your receipts and financial entries locally. You can delete individual entries anytime inside the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing application storage permanently deletes all local records immediately.
Technical Logs & Crash Reports Secure Developer Console Platforms (Google Play Console) Maximum of 90 days Used to identify and fix technical crashes. Automatically deleted or aggregated/anonymized by Google Play Console after 90 days.
Customer Support Communications Secure Email Server Archive Maximum of 180 days Maintains support correspondence to resolve your inquiries. Permanently deleted within 180 days following the resolution of your request, unless required by legal compliance.

If you wish to request the manual deletion of any customer support correspondence before the standard 180-day retention period expires, you may submit a request to the support contact in Section 8 at any time.

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data. Anonymized crash metrics are handled only through trusted developer platforms (such as Google Developer Services) to maintain application stability, subject to stringent security requirements. We may only disclose information if required under strict legal obligations or valid governmental requests.

5. Children's Privacy

Pocket Receipt is a general audience utility application intended for personal expense and budget management. We do not knowingly target or collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information via customer support email, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly delete the data.

6. Your Choices and Rights

Regardless of your geographic location, you have full control over your data:

  • Device Data Control: You can view, edit, or delete any receipt photo or expense entry directly within the application at any time, or clear all app data in your device system settings.
  • Access and Erasure: You have the right to request access to or permanent deletion of any customer support emails or messages you have sent us. We will honor all valid requests within 30 days.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect application improvements, new features, or regulatory updates. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date. We recommend checking this page periodically.
